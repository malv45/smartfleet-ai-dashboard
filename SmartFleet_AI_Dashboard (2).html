Experiment Design and Evaluation

Hypothesis: AI-assisted route recommendations and early risk flags can improve decision quality versus baseline dispatch in a simulated fleet.

Independent variables: Traffic disruption, driver availability, and whether route optimization is enabled. Outputs: Modeled on-time delivery, operating costs, maintenance risk, and staffing coverage.

Method: Run the baseline at 20% traffic disruption and 21 drivers; then enable AI-assisted routing without changing the other inputs. Repeat under 40% traffic disruption and 18 drivers. Record cost and service changes for each paired scenario.

Limitation: Results are calculated from assumptions, not observed outcomes. This prototype tests dashboard logic and decision-support usability; it cannot establish real-world AI effectiveness. A next-stage pilot would use approved, anonymized operational data and compare actual outcomes with a control group.
